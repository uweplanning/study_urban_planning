    Planning personas and school subjects matrix

    Explore in three ways. By my subjects starts from the subjects you are studying and shows which personas they lead to. By persona shows how strongly each A level and T level subject links to the personas you choose. By school project starts from the projects you enjoyed. Whichever you pick, you see up to three personas at a time.

    Choose the A levels you are studying, or your T level. A T level is worth three A levels, so it can be chosen on its own.
